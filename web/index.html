Key figures

Average daily values per month

Orientation comparison

Annual values for the panel's orientation, the best orientations and the ones to compare; azimuth as compass angle (180 = south).

Sky hemisphere

Annual radiation per sky patch (direct normal plus diffuse from the patch), independent of the panel orientation; obstructed patches dimmed, their border outlined in cyan. Tap or hover over a patch for its values; to pan or zoom, switch the tools on in the plot's toolbar.

Average daily profile per month

Radiation on the panel in W/m² over the hour of the day: total (black), direct (orange) and diffuse (blue), each unobstructed (dashed) and obstructed (solid).

Radiation per day

Total radiation on the panel per day: unobstructed (light) behind obstructed (dark).

Carpet plots

Hourly total radiation on the panel over the year: hour of the day across, day of the year downwards; unobstructed and obstructed side by side (on a phone, one below the other).

Panel orientation

Annual radiation on the panel over azimuth and tilt, unobstructed and obstructed; star: best orientation, circle: the panel's; dotted lines enclose 95 % and 90 % of the best.

PV yield estimator

Photovoltaic yield from weather data and an obstructed sky description; all computation runs in your browser (Python via Pyodide). Only the site search and the weather download contact other services.

Steps: set the site and the weather (Site & weather), optionally describe the obstructed sky from a LiDAR scan, photos or a sky map (Obstruction), set the panel (Panel), then open the Results: they are computed when needed.

Loaded inputs and the form are kept in this browser for your next visit (photos and the point cloud only until the page is closed or reloaded). To keep everything or move it to another device, save the project (menu ☰: one zip with all inputs and the matching results) and load it there; single files (weather, obstructed sky description, photos, config) can be saved and loaded as well.

Source code and CLI: github.com/laggondo/pv_yield. Sample weather data: PVGIS © European Union, 2001–2026. Downloaded weather data: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). The first visit downloads about 60 MB (Pyodide, pvlib, pandas, bokeh), later visits use the browser cache.
